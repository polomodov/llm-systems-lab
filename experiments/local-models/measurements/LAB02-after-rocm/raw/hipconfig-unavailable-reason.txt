hipconfig is not part of ROCm 7.2 user-mode apt packages.
A grep across https://repo.radeon.com/rocm/apt/7.2/dists/noble/main/binary-amd64/Packages.gz
returned no Package: hipconfig line. The utility has been removed from the
ROCm 7.x distribution. Equivalent information can be obtained from
/opt/rocm-7.2.0/lib/libamdhip64.so.7.2.70200 or hipcc --version after
installing rocm-hip-runtime-dev (not required for LAB02).

For LAB02 this field is filled as unavailable with this reason. The LAB02
hardware passport is satisfied by rocminfo alone, which enumerates three HSA
agents (CPU, GPU=gfx1151, DSP=aie2p) and reports full pool/clock/BDF info.
